I am a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of Amsterdam. My research sits where **explainable AI** meets **operations research**. Optimization models and machine-learning models increasingly drive real decisions, yet the people who rely on them rarely see _why_ a solution looks the way it does. I develop methods that make these systems transparent, so that their decisions can be understood, checked and trusted.

Togethether with my team we collect and promote recent research on explanaible optimization: expopt.org

My work combines mathematical analysis with computational experiments, mostly in Python with Gurobi.
